# Sources and reuse

The historical figures are attributed to © State of Victoria (Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action), Biodiversity 2037 Progress Update 2020–2022, printed pages 5, 7, 11, 15 and 19:
https://www.environment.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0025/722563/Bio2037-Progress-update-2020-2022-final.pdf

The CSV is a transcription of the numeric tables. Shares, target attainment and gaps are derived calculations; the dashboard is a student interpretation, not an official departmental product. Report photographs, logos and narrative case studies are not redistributed.

DEECA's website copyright notice states CC BY 4.0 for departmental material, excluding images/branding and third-party content:
https://www.deeca.vic.gov.au/copyright
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Spatial data come from the public NatureKit SMP4 summary-area service identified in README.md. The local handoff includes the frozen display cache; it is excluded from the Git repository. No new licence is asserted over source data. Dataset-specific spatial redistribution terms remain to be confirmed before broader redistribution. Source attributes are preserved; only display geometry is simplified. The workbook contains only historical numeric activity records, not spatial geometry.
